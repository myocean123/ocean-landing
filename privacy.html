Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 5, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 5, 2026

01 Introduction

Welcome to XZORE ("we," "our," or "us"). XZORE is a professional networking and career platform designed to connect job seekers and employers, with a focus on India's workforce. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

By using XZORE, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

02 Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and password when you register.
  • Profile Information: Work experience, education, skills, location, profile photo, and career preferences.
  • Camera & Media: We access your camera and photo library solely to allow you to upload a profile photo or documents. We do not store camera data beyond what you explicitly upload.
  • Location Data: Approximate location to show relevant job opportunities near you (only when permitted).
  • Usage Data: App interactions, pages viewed, features used, and device information for analytics and improvement.
  • Communications: Messages sent through the platform between users.

03 How We Use Your Information

We use collected data to:

  • Create and manage your XZORE account
  • Connect job seekers with relevant employers and opportunities
  • Personalize job recommendations and network suggestions
  • Send notifications about applications, messages, and platform updates
  • Improve app performance, security, and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

04 Camera Permission

XZORE requests access to your device camera for the following purposes only:

  • Taking or uploading a profile photo
  • Uploading resume documents or certificates from your gallery
📸 We never record video, capture images in the background, or store any camera data without your explicit action. Camera access is requested only at the moment you choose to upload a photo.

05 Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information with:

  • Employers/Recruiters: Profile information you make public or submit via job applications.
  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party services (AWS, analytics providers) that help us operate the platform under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law, court order, or to protect user safety.

06 Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), secure cloud infrastructure (AWS), and access controls to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

07 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, update, or delete your personal data through the app settings
  • Withdraw consent for optional permissions (e.g., camera, location) at any time via device settings
  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Request account deletion by contacting us

08 Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. Certain data may be retained for legal or fraud-prevention purposes.

09 Children's Privacy

XZORE is not intended for users under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email. Continued use of XZORE after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
